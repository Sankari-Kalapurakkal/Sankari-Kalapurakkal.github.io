As a designated Member of the Institution of Engineering and Technology (MIET), my academic research and technical expertise focus heavily on embedded systems design, control loop programming, and real-world system architecture optimization.

🎓 Core Competencies & Technical Skills
* Hardware & Systems: Microcontrollers (Arduino, STM32, TI DSPs), Sensors & Signal Conditioning, Embedded Control Loops.
* Software Frameworks: MATLAB / Simulink, C/C++, Altium Designer, Multisim, Git.
* Control Systems: Fuzzy Logic Design, Intelligent Systems Engineering, Signal Processing.

🚀 Featured Research & Projects

* Overview: Engineered a complete hardware-software co-design tracking system using intelligent classification systems to analyze biometric parameters. 
* Methodology: Implemented real-time sensor processing and designed discrete Fuzzy Logic Algorithms to interpret telemetry metrics accurately.
* Impact: Delivered high-precision dynamic balancing tracking, helping isolate specific stability markers for biomedical applications.
