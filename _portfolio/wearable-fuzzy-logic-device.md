Developed as part of my Master's curriculum at Sheffield Hallam University under the academic supervision of Prof. Dr. Reza Saatchi, this project bridges advanced digital signal processing with intelligent classification frameworks.

### 🛠️ Core Engineering Tasks
* **Sensor Fusion & Calibration:** Integrated an MPU6050 inertial measurement unit (IMU) to capture multi-axis acceleration and angular velocity metrics tracking human mobility variations.
* **On-Chip Decision Architecture:** Modelled and deployed an embedded Mamdani Fuzzy Logic inference engine to process real-time posture datasets.
* **Performance Metrics:** Optimized the low-latency hardware control loop, reaching **90% prediction accuracy** and **100% sensitivity** metrics during real-world fall verification models.
* **Technology Stack:** MATLAB, Simulink, Embedded C++, Microcontroller Pipelines (Arduino/STM32 platforms).
