During my Master's studies at Sheffield Hallam University, I had the privilege of participating in the UK Environment Agency Intern Programme within the Regulated Industry sector. Coming from an electrical and electronics engineering background with prior experience in power utility infrastructure, my goal was to discover how engineering practices intertwine with environmental sustainability and net-zero targets.

### Key Highlights of My Journey:
* **Regulatory Sector Frameworks:** Worked closely under my line manager to understand the critical relationship between active industrial growth and local environmental well-being.
* **Operational Field Dynamics:** Took part in site audits at regional infrastructure locations and shadowed specialists dealing with compliance management.
* **Core Takeaways:** Ventured far outside my technical comfort zone, developed robust professional networking strategies, and reinforced my personal engineering pledge towards a greener future.
