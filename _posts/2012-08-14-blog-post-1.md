During my time as a Master's student at Sheffield Hallam University, I had the privilege of joining the UK Environment Agency as a Summer Intern within the Regulated Industries department. 

This post details my hands-on experiences navigating the intersection of electrical engineering, regulatory compliance, and environmental sustainability.

### Key Highlights:
* **Regulatory Compliance:** Supported the team in ensuring sustainable compliance with environmental standards across industrial sectors.
* **Field Work:** Participated in vital site visits, data tracking collection, and comprehensive team shadowing alongside environmental specialists.
* **Carbon Literacy:** Completed comprehensive carbon management and sustainability practices tracking.
